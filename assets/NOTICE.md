# Third-party data notices

## Spelling dictionary

The `en_GB-ise.dic` and `en_GB-ise.aff` Hunspell dictionary files are generated from SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists) by Kevin Atkinson, from wordlist.aspell.net (https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist), release 2026.02.25. They are used under the SCOWL licence, which is permissive and requires that the copyright and licence notice are kept with the data. See the SCOWL repository for the full copyright and licence text.

## Word frequencies and n-grams

`freq_en_GB.txt` and `ngram.bin` (the word n-gram model, built from the sentences of the same corpus) is derived from word and sentence data of the Leipzig Corpora Collection, corpus `eng-uk_web-public_2018_1M`, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The data has been filtered (to words valid in en_GB-ise), aggregated by lower-case form and truncated. Citation:

D. Goldhahn, T. Eckart and U. Quasthoff (2012): Building Large Monolingual Dictionaries at the Leipzig Corpora Collection: From 100 to 200 Languages. In: Proceedings of the 8th International Language Resources and Evaluation Conference (LREC 2012).
